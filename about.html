Who We Are

The HMA Alumni Association brings together former students of Haile-Manas Academy to stay connected with one another and with their school.

We serve as a bridge between alumni, faculty and administration, creating opportunities for regular communication, networking and collaboration.

Through education, hard work and dedication, our community aims to support graduates and inspire future generations of students.

Our Objective

To build and maintain lifelong connections between HMA alumni, faculty and administration through communication, networking and shared support for the HMA community.

What We Aim to Do

Connect Alumni

Encourage regular communication, reconnect classmates and build personal and professional networks among HMA graduates.

Give Back to HMA

Provide a platform for graduates to support school development, including improvements to roads, the library, laboratories and cafeteria.

Support Students

Share experiences, provide mentorship and help students explore educational and career opportunities.

Our Shared Values

Education

We believe in education as a foundation for growth, opportunity and a better future.

Hard Work & Dedication

We encourage effort, commitment and determination in pursuing our dreams.

Community & Service

We support one another and give back through our time, knowledge and experience.

From Achieving Dreams to Supporting Others

Students come to HMA with dreams. As graduates pursue those dreams, they can help others do the same by sharing their journeys and supporting the school community.

Every alumnus has something to contribute: a story, a connection, advice or an opportunity. Together, these contributions help build a lasting relationship with HMA.
