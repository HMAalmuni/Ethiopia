Who We Are

The HMA Alumni Association brings together former students of Haile-Manas Academy to stay connected with one another and with their school.

We serve as a bridge between graduates, faculty and administration, creating opportunities to exchange information, share experiences and contribute to the HMA community.

Our connection continues beyond graduation through friendship, professional networks and a shared commitment to helping others achieve their dreams.

Our Objective

To build and maintain lifelong connections between HMA alumni, faculty and administration through regular communication, networking and support for the school and its students.

What We Aim to Do

Communication & Connection

Provide a bridge for regular information exchange between alumni and HMA, helping graduates stay informed and connected.

Support School Development

Create opportunities for graduates to support improvements to school facilities, including roads, the library, laboratories and cafeteria.

Mentorship & Opportunities

Encourage alumni to share their knowledge, guide students and connect the community with learning and career opportunities.

What Brings Us Together

Education

A shared belief in education as a foundation for opportunity and personal growth.

Hard Work & Dedication

Encouraging one another to pursue our goals with effort, commitment and determination.

Giving Back

Helping future generations dream, learn and succeed by contributing our time, experience and support.

Your Journey Is Part of Our Story

Every graduate has experiences to share. Whether you are continuing your education, starting a career or serving your community, your journey can inspire fellow alumni and current students.

Stay connected, share your achievements and take part in building the HMA alumni community.

Stay Connected. Give Back. Inspire.

Your relationship with HMA does not end at graduation. Be part of a community that supports one another and the next generation.
