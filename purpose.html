Our Purpose

Connecting generations, supporting HMA and helping others achieve their dreams.

Winning Life Through Education

Our Objective

To build and maintain lifelong connections between HMA alumni, faculty and administration through networking and meaningful engagement.

How We Fulfil Our Purpose

1. Regular Communication

Serve as a bridge for constant and effective information exchange between HMA alumni, faculty and administration.

2. Fundraising & Institutional Advancement

Establish a platform for graduates to support HMA’s development, including roads, the library, laboratories and cafeteria. Students come to HMA with dreams, achieve their dreams and help others dream and succeed through financial and other forms of support.

3. Mentorship & Career Guidance

Provide opportunities for graduates to motivate and inspire new students through mentorship and career guidance.

4. Internships & Job Opportunities

Help establish internship and job opportunities for alumni members through connections within the alumni community.

5. Alumni Events

Organize regular professional networking and community service events that provide opportunities for alumni to reconnect, make new friends and enjoy shared memories of their time at HMA.

6. Alumni Records & Global Impact

Compile alumni profiles and maintain a database of their success stories and impact. Champion HMA’s contribution globally by sharing, celebrating and promoting alumni achievements.

Achieve Your Dreams. Help Others Achieve Theirs.

Stay connected with HMA and contribute your knowledge, experience, connections and support to the next generation.
